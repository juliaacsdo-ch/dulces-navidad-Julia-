## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the legacy, warmth, and quiet luxury of an ancestral holiday confectionary workshop. It evokes nostalgia, family tradition, and artisanal mastery through a tactile, editorial aesthetic that balances historic confectionary packaging with contemporary haute patisserie digital retail.

### Audience & Mood
- **Target Audience:** Connoisseurs of traditional gastronomy, holiday gift-givers seeking distinguished curated selections, and families valuing authentic culinary heritage.
- **Emotional Response:** Comfort, sensory warmth, anticipation of holiday festivities, and confidence in unmatched ingredient quality.

### Design Style: Editorial Tactile & Warm Elegance
The visual language pairs deliberate, book-style editorial hierarchy with warm physical confectionary metaphors. It rejects stark white digital interfaces in favor of soft flour-and-sugar cream canvases, deep festive burgundy tones, muted heritage gold accents, and delicate gilded rules that honor centuries-old wrapping and boxcraft.

## Colors

The palette reproduces the tactile richness of festive baking: deep wine preserves, antique gilded tissue, warm unbleached parchment, and roasted almond undertones.

### Color Roles & Hierarchy
- **Canvas Base (`#FAF6F0`):** Soft, warm confectioner's cream; reduces eye fatigue and establishes an organic, artisanal foundation.
- **Elevated Canvas (`#F3EBDD`):** Rich parchment tone used for secondary product surfaces, gift trays, and structural section blocks.
- **Primary Brand Accent (`#7D1424`):** Regal deep burgundy used for high-impact brand accents, primary buttons, and focal typography.
- **Deep Heritage Accent (`#5C0E1B`):** Dark crimson reserved for interactive hover states, formal header bars, and decorative foil bands.
- **Metallic Gold Accent (`#D4AF37` / `#C59B27`):** Fine filigree borders, rating stars, award laurels, vintage seal emblems, and badge rims. Never used for large blocks of text.
- **Soft Charcoal Ink (`#2B1E1E`):** Warm, espresso-tinted dark charcoal for long-form reading, weights, and ingredient declarations, avoiding the harshness of pure black.
- **Pristine White (`#FFFFFF`):** High-light surface reserved strictly for elevated product cards, packaging zoom containers, and modal dialogs.

## Typography

The typographic balance bridges historic artisanal branding with contemporary conversion clarity.

### Hierarchy & Usage
- **Headlines (`Playfair Display`):** Carries the literary pedigree of classic holiday typography. Headlines use italic treatments selectively for artisan batch descriptions, historical vintages, and craft origins.
- **Interface & Text (`Plus Jakarta Sans`):** Delivers clean geometry and open apertures for product technical sheets, allergen lists, ingredient transparency, prices, and high-legibility checkout flows.
- **Labels & Micro-copy:** Formatted in uppercase with extended tracking (`0.08em` to `0.1em`) to mimic stamped box labels and traditional confectionery metal seals.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout philosophy uses a disciplined 12-column grid system paired with intentional negative space, echoing high-end editorial books and confectionary catalogs.

### Breakpoints & Adaptability
- **Desktop (1200px+):** Max-width container capped at `1280px` centered, utilizing `margin: 3rem` with `gutter: 1.5rem`. Multi-item gift hampers and product grids sit comfortably in 3- or 4-column structures.
- **Tablet (768px - 1199px):** 8-column layout with `margin: 2rem` and `gutter: 1.25rem`. Two-column arrangement for catalog cards with full-width showcase rows.
- **Mobile (< 768px):** 4-column layout with `margin-mobile: 1.25rem` and `gutter-mobile: 1rem`. High-density single-column and horizontal-carousel product displays allow easy thumb scanning of pastry collections.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual depth avoids cool, clinical dropshadows in favor of soft, radiant warmth reminiscent of fireplace glow and warm display counter lighting.

### Shadow Characteristics
- **Resting Surface (Level 1):** `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(43, 30, 30, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(125, 20, 36, 0.03)`
- **Interactive Card Hover (Level 2):** `0 10px 24px -4px rgba(43, 30, 30, 0.08), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.12)`
- **Floating Modals & Hampers (Level 3):** `0 20px 38px -6px rgba(43, 30, 30, 0.14), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(92, 14, 27, 0.08)`

### Borders & Delimiters
Separators use subtle double-lines or hairline rules (`1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.35)`) and parchment seam lines (`1px solid rgba(43, 30, 30, 0.08)`), emulating gilded box liners and artisanal wrapping papers.

## Shapes

The design uses a soft roundedness (`0.25rem` / `4px` base) that evokes handmade paper boxes, gold-embossed confection sleeves, and cut pastry trays without straying into overly casual or toy-like curved geometries.

### Geometry Details
- **Buttons and Inputs:** `0.25rem` (4px) corner radius, preserving a structured and traditional presence.
- **Product and Recipe Cards:** `0.5rem` (8px), offering a tailored, clean container.
- **Badges and Seals:** Fully circular or delicately octagonal clipped corners to emulate authentic bakery wax seals and holiday award ribbons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Action (e.g., "Añadir a la Cesta", "Comprar Colección"):** Background in `#7D1424` with text in `#FAF6F0`, subtle 1px border in `#C59B27`, and rounded radius of `4px`. Hover state transitions to `#5C0E1B` with a refined gold glow shadow.
- **Secondary Action (e.g., "Personalizar Surtido"):** Transparent background with `1.5px solid #7D1424`, text in `#7D1424`. Hover shifts to `#FAF6F0` filled state with warm cream contrast.
- **Artisan Text Button:** Text in `#7D1424`, accompanied by an underline in `#D4AF37` offset by 4px.

### Product Cards
- **Structure:** Crisp `#FFFFFF` card surface wrapped in a subtle `1px solid rgba(212, 175, 55, 0.25)` frame.
- **Imagery:** Warm studio photography of polvorones wrapped in classic tissue silk and artisan mantecados dusted with powdered sugar, resting on natural stone or festive linens.
- **Content:** Product designation in `headline-sm` Playfair Display, weight designation (e.g., "Caja 500g") in muted label style, price prominently emphasized in `Plus Jakarta Sans Bold` (`#2B1E1E`), accompanied by a discrete gold seal icon for designation of origin (e.g., "I.G.P. Estepa").

### Chips & Badges
- **Heritage Badges (e.g., "Receta Tradicional 1890", "Edición Limitada"):** Background `#F3EBDD`, border `1px solid #D4AF37`, typography in uppercase `#7D1424` (`label-sm`).
- **Allergen & Ingredient Chips:** Soft neutral backgrounds (`rgba(43, 30, 30, 0.05)`), typography in `#2B1E1E`, displaying gluten, almond, or lard specifications with micro-icons.

### Input Fields & Controls
- **Text Inputs:** Background `#FFFFFF` set against the `#FAF6F0` canvas. Border `1px solid rgba(43, 30, 30, 0.18)`. Focus states elevate the field with `1.5px solid #7D1424` and a soft warm tint. Placeholder text in `#2B1E1E` at 40% opacity.
- **Checkboxes & Radios:** Accented in `#7D1424` with golden focus halos. Checked icons render in warm parchment `#FAF6F0`.

### Specialized Holiday Confectionery Components
- **Gift Box Assortment Builder:** Interactive module allowing customers to compose custom combinations of mantecados de canela, polvorones de almendra, roscos de vino, and alfajores. Units display counter chips with gilded borders.
- **Artisan Batch Certificate:** A specialized banner rendered in `#F3EBDD` with a double hairline border in `#D4AF37`, displaying the batch number, master baker signature in script, and packaging date.
- **Tasting Notes & Heritage Drawer:** An expandable accordion featuring parchment background styling, detailing almond roast profiles, spice blends (cinnamon, sesame, anise), and historical origins.
